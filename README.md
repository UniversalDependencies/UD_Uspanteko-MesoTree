# Summary

The MesoTree Uspanteko UD treebank consists of a token-balanced set of trees drawn dictionary example sentences and spoken narratives.

# Introduction

The MesoTree Uspanteko UD treebank incorporates data from two sources, namely the example sentences from the 2007 dictionary by Méndez, produced under the auspicies of Oxlajuuj Keej Mayab' Ajtz'iib' (OKMA). The narrative portion of the corpus come from those collected by Robert Henderson, Ryan Bennet, and Tomás Méndez López (Henderson and Bennett 2026). The later cover personal narratives, but also traditional stories and local history, for example, sources for toponyms.


# Acknowledgments

We would like to thank the people of Uspantán without whom this project would not have been possible. We would also especially like to recognize Virgilio Tomás Méndez who passed away during the creation of this corpus. He was an educator, former president of the Uspanteko branch of the Academy of Mayan Languages, and a great defender of the Uspanteko language. A story he related, Xoqoneb', is one of the many that form part of this corpus.
